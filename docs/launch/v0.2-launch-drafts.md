## X thread (English)
1/ Every AI I use forgets me, and each one remembers a different version of me. So I built Mnemos: one memory you
own, shared by Claude, ChatGPT and Cursor. Now it's a Mac app. No Docker, no terminal. 🧵
2/ Download, right-click → Open, type your name, click "Connect to Claude Desktop". From then on Claude checks your
memory and saves what matters, without being asked.
3/ You can see and fix what it knows: search, edit, pin, mark as outdated, forget (with undo), a graph of people and
projects, and your skills.
4/ Bring what you already have: import your ChatGPT export, what Claude remembers about you, or a folder of notes.
5/ It keeps itself tidy. It flags duplicates when saving and runs a nightly cleanup that suggests merges and
contradictions. You decide. Ollama is optional.
6/ Separate contexts (work / personal) never mix. Everything stays on your Mac. MIT, open source → [link]
