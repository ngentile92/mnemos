# Friend test: first run without help

Goal: someone who has never used Mnemos goes from download to "a second AI knew what I told the first one" in
under 5 minutes, with **zero help**. Timing is kept on their computer only (Settings → Advanced → First-run timing).

## Before
- A Mac with at least one AI app installed (Claude Desktop, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex or VS Code), never connected to Mnemos.
- The latest release from GitHub (`Mnemos.dmg`). Do not explain anything beyond the line below.

## Say only this
> "This app gives all your AIs one shared memory. Install it and get it working. I'll just watch."

## Watch (do not help; write down every hesitation)
1. Opens the app. Note anything confusing on first launch (security warning, password).
2. Welcome: types a name (optional) and presses Start.
3. Pick your AIs: presses Connect on a detected app, restarts it, sees "Connected ✓".
4. Home → "See it work": says the sentence in one AI, asks in another; all three steps tick.
5. Optional: Skills → "Try it in your AI" on one example.

## After
- Settings → Advanced → First-run timing: write down the minutes to "First AI app connected" and "aha".
- Pass: aha under 5 minutes and no help given. Every hesitation becomes an issue labelled `mvp`.
